## `glossary.md`

`glossary.md` is a Markdown copy of the dogwhistle glossary of Mendelsohn
et al. (2023), included so the pipeline runs without a separate download:

> Julia Mendelsohn, Ronan Le Bras, Yejin Choi, and Maarten Sap. 2023.
> [From Dogwhistles to Bullhorns: Unveiling Coded Rhetoric with Language
> Models](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.845). In *Proceedings of
> ACL 2023*, pages 15162–15180.

The glossary is published at [dogwhistles.allen.ai](https://dogwhistles.allen.ai).
It is not covered by this repository's CC0 license: it belongs to its
authors, and its descriptions and examples quote the sources each entry
cites (including RationalWiki and Wikipedia, which are licensed CC BY-SA).
Please cite the paper above if you use it.
